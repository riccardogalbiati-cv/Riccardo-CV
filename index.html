About me

Early-career consumer insights and market research graduate with an MSc in Media & Creative Industries and a BSc in Economics & Business. Hands-on experience designing surveys in Qualtrics, analysing quantitative data in SPSS and translating results into clear research conclusions. Experienced in qualitative interviews, audience research and applying insights to campaign positioning. Interested in rapid, practical research that helps brands test ideas and make informed decisions.
